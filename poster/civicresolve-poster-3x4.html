CivicResolve AI

From citizen complaint to verified civic resolution — intelligently classified, routed, tracked and explained.

AI Civic Complaint-to-Resolution Intelligence Platform

🚧 The Challenge

Complaints about roads, drainage, waste, water, streetlights and public facilities are often incomplete, duplicated, sent to the wrong department and difficult to follow up. Citizens need transparency; officials need actionable intelligence.

🤖 Our Solution

A multi-agent civic service platform that understands text, photos and GPS, then turns every report into a prioritized case with a responsible department, SLA, work order, progress evidence and citizen-friendly resolution explanation.

04 Government Official Agent — operational intelligence

📋 For every case

Shows the full complaint ID, citizen evidence, GPS, category, 2,000-word agent description, official summary, department, team, priority, duplicates, work order, timeline and next action.

🛠️ For every update

The officer can claim a location-matched case, record remarks, update status, upload progress/resolution photos, review the agent’s completion estimate and notify the citizen after verified resolution.
